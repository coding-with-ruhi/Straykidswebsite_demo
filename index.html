About Stray Kids

Stray Kids debuted in 2018 under JYP Entertainment, but their story began long before that. Built on authenticity, resilience, and self-production, they created their own path in the music industry. Their music speaks about youth, confusion, strength, vulnerability, ambition, and growth. Every lyric feels personal. Every stage feels powerful. Every era feels like evolution. To STAYs, Stray Kids are more than artists. They are comfort on difficult days. They are motivation when the world feels heavy. They are proof that being different is powerful. And that is why we STAY.
